Working without internet

Every aid on this page is a single file that runs in your browser. Nothing is loaded from the internet while it runs.

To keep a copy: open the page, press Ctrl and S, and save it to your own folder. It will work later with the network switched off.

Slides, student notes, lab data and SQL scripts are in the repository: github.com/nirangadh/Data-Warehousing-and-Business-Intelligence
